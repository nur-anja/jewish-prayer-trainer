# Prefix letters (Moshe ve-Khalev)

Some short words are written as a single letter attached to the front of the next word. The mnemonic "Moses and Caleb" spells out most of them:

| Mnemonic | Meaning |
|---|---|
| מֹשֶׁה וְכָלֵב | Moses and Caleb |

| Prefix | Meaning | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| וְ | and | <b style="color:red">וְ</b>אָהַבְתָּ | and you shall love |
| בְּ | in, with | <b style="color:red">בְּ</b>כָל | with all |
| לְ | to, for | <b style="color:red">לְ</b>עוֹלָם | forever (to the world) |
| כְּ | like, as | <b style="color:red">כָּ</b>מוֹךָ | like you |
| מִ | from | <b style="color:red">מֵ</b>עוֹלָם | from eternity |
| הַ | the | <b style="color:red">הַ</b>גָּדוֹל | the great |
| שֶׁ | that, who | <b style="color:red">שֶׁ</b>עָשָׂה | who made |

The vowel under the prefix shifts depending on the next letter, but the letter stays the same.

## Stacking

Prefixes can stack. "And" always comes first.

| Example | Breakdown |
|---|---|
| <b style="color:red">וּבְ</b>כָל | and + in + all |

## "The" hiding inside in, to, like

After the "in", "to" and "like" prefixes, the ה of "the" drops out. Only its vowel stays, usually a patach, so the vowel under the prefix tells you "the" is there:

| Example | Translation |
|---|---|
| <b style="color:red">בְּ</b>שָׁמַיִם | in heaven (no "the") |
| <b style="color:red">בַּ</b>שָּׁמַיִם | in the heavens |

## Watch out

A word starting with one of these letters doesn't always have a prefix. The letter may be part of the root, as in the word for king below. Strip a letter only if what's left is a real word.

| Example | Translation |
|---|---|
| מֶלֶךְ | king (the מ is part of the root) |
