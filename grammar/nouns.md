# Nouns built from roots

Many nouns are a root poured into a template. Recognizing the template shows the root.

## מ prefix

A מ prefix often means "the place, thing or result of" the root:

| Example | Root | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| <b style="color:red">מָ</b>קוֹם | קום | place (where one stands) |
| <b style="color:red">מִ</b>צְוָה | צוה | commandment |
| <b style="color:red">מִ</b>שְׁפָּט | שפט | judgment |
| <b style="color:red">מִ</b>קְדָּשׁ | קדש | sanctuary |
| <b style="color:red">מַ</b>עֲשֶׂה | עשה | deed |

## ת prefix

A ת prefix also forms nouns, often naming an act or its result:

| Example | Root | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| <b style="color:red">תְּ</b>פִלָּה | פלל | prayer |
| <b style="color:red">תְּ</b>הִלָּה | הלל | praise |
| <b style="color:red">תּ</b>וֹרָה | ירה | teaching |
| <b style="color:red">תְּ</b>שׁוּבָה | שוב | return, repentance |

## ה ending for abstract nouns

Nouns with a kubutz or shuruk before the last root letter and a ה at the end often name a quality or an act:

| Example | Root | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| גְּאֻלָּ<b style="color:red">ה</b> | גאל | redemption |
| גְּבוּרָ<b style="color:red">ה</b> | גבר | might |
| יְשׁוּעָ<b style="color:red">ה</b> | ישע | salvation |
| קְדֻשָּׁ<b style="color:red">ה</b> | קדש | holiness |

## ות ending for abstract nouns

A וּת ending also makes abstract nouns:

| Example | Root | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| מַלְכ<b style="color:red">וּת</b> | מלך | kingship |

## Dagesh and ו for acts

Nouns with a hiriq under the first root letter, a dagesh in the second and a ו with shuruk before the last often name an act:

| Example | Root | Note | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| קִדּ<b style="color:red">וּ</b>שׁ | קדש | dagesh in ד | sanctification, Kiddush |
| לִמּ<b style="color:red">וּ</b>ד | למד | dagesh in מ | learning |
| סִפּ<b style="color:red">וּ</b>ר | ספר | dagesh in פ | telling, story |

They are the nouns of verbs with a dagesh in the middle root letter, like "sanctifies" in [participle](participle.md).

## Watch out

A מ at the start can also be a [participle](participle.md) or the [prefix letter](prefix-letters.md) "from". A ת at the start can also be a [future tense](future-tense.md) prefix.

A ו before the last root letter also comes in passive participles, but there the first root letter has a kamatz and the second no dagesh:

| Example | Root | Note | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּת<b style="color:red">וּ</b>ב | כתב | passive participle | written |
| קִדּ<b style="color:red">וּ</b>שׁ | קדש | noun | sanctification |
